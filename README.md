### About me

I design and ship AI systems end to end: from the data pipelines that feed them and the models that power them, to the APIs that expose them and the cloud infrastructure they run on. I care about turning complex data into reliable, useful systems.

- 🏛️ &nbsp; Currently at the **Institute of Statistical and Geographic Information of Jalisco (IIEG)**, building data infrastructure and AI solutions
- 🤖 &nbsp; Building **AI solutions end to end**, from data to deployed models
- 🗄️ &nbsp; Database design, data warehousing and geospatial data
- 🛠️ &nbsp; I build side projects: small tools, automations and experiments that make my desk a little less chaotic
